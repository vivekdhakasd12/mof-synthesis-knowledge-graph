**Topic:** *Benchmarking Large Language Models for Knowledge Graph Construction from Materials Science Literature*

**In brief:** The project develops an end-to-end pipeline that uses Large Language Models (GPT-4o, Claude, Llama-3) to extract Material–Synthesis–Property–Application relations from open-access materials science publications, stores them in a Neo4j knowledge graph with full provenance back to source papers, and rigorously benchmarks LLM-based extraction against established domain-specific baselines (ChemDataExtractor and MatSciBERT). The final deliverables are a populated knowledge graph, a Streamlit dashboard supporting natural-language-to-Cypher querying, and an evaluation report comparing precision, recall, and F1 across extractors, prompting strategies (zero-shot, few-shot, schema-guided, chain-of-thought), and paper sections.

The attached five-page exposé (`expose.docx`) covers the working title, research question and sub-questions, the state of the art (thirteen references), methodology, the twelve-week work plan, and expected results — all in SRH format.
